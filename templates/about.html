About Vélocity

Built by drivers. Driven by obsession.

Vélocity started as a spreadsheet of cars we couldn't stop talking about. It grew into a place to discover, compare and obsess over the machines that make driving feel like something worth remembering.

Our Story

We were tired of car listings that read like inventory sheets — rows of numbers with none of the feeling. So a small team of engineers, photographers and lifelong car obsessives built the site we wished existed: one that treats every car like the engineering marvel it is.

It started small — a shared spreadsheet, a handful of cars we couldn't stop arguing about, and way too many browser tabs open to manufacturer spec sheets at 2am. What we kept coming back to was simple: the numbers only tell half the story. A 0–60 time doesn't capture the sound of a flat-six waking up, and a horsepower figure doesn't explain why some cars get remembered and others just get driven. So we set out to build a place that respects both — the spec sheet and the feeling.

How we work

Nothing goes live by accident.

01

Source

We pull specs directly from manufacturer documentation — never third-party estimates or forum guesswork.

02

Verify

Every figure gets cross-checked against at least two independent sources before it's allowed near the site.

03

Shoot

Where we can, we photograph the car ourselves. Where we can't, we credit whoever did — always.

04

Publish

Nothing goes live until someone who actually loves cars has personally signed off on it.
