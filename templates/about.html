About Vélocity

Built by drivers. Driven by obsession.

Vélocity started as a spreadsheet of cars we couldn't stop talking about. It grew into a place to discover, compare and obsess over the machines that make driving feel like something worth remembering.

Our Story

We were tired of car listings that read like inventory sheets — rows of numbers with none of the feeling. So a small team of engineers, photographers and lifelong car obsessives built the site we wished existed: one that treats every car like the engineering marvel it is.
